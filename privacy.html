Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

HaMoreh Tutor App ("the app") is a private tool used by a single person, its owner, to manage their own tutoring work. It is not available to the public.

Google user data

With the owner's consent, the app accesses the owner's Google account through Google APIs:

  • Google Calendar: to read and create lesson events in the owner's calendars.
  • Google Sheets: to read and update the owner's lesson spreadsheet.
  • Gmail (send only): to send the owner a daily summary email.

This data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data is stored only in the owner's own accounts and services. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google account settings, which stops all access.
